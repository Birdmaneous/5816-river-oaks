Highlights

Good quality office/warehouse, built in 1992

Warehouse

4,378 SF with two 12′ x 14′ overhead roll-up loading doors (one front, one rear) and a 16′ eave height. Ceiling and perimeter walls are insulated with fiberglass batts.
